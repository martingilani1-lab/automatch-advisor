SAFETY EQUIPMENT PER TRIM — fill the gaps (2026-10-26)

Same idea as the AC list: write each item ONLY on the trim where it first becomes standard
(higher trims inherit it), "(opt)" if it's optional on that trim.
Use these names so they link to the NCAP explanations:
  ABS · ESP · airbags (e.g. "Dual front and side airbags", "6 airbags", "Front, side and curtain airbags")
  Front Assist · Lane Assist · Driver fatigue detection · Adaptive cruise control (ACC) · Travel Assist
  Rear parking sensors · Front and rear parking sensors · Park Assist · Matrix LED headlights
Format:  trim: item, item (opt), item
"Have" = what the DB already has. Leave a car empty if you have nothing. "-" = no trims in DB.
